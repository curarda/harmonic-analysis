HARMONIC ANALYSIS

Privacy

Plain-language summary of what this tool keeps. The short version: nothing.

What happens in your browser

The MIDI files you drop are read and analysed entirely on your own device. The files are not uploaded anywhere.

What is saved

Nothing. This site has no server-side storage, no accounts, and no analytics that collect your analysis. Closing the page discards everything.

Questions or a removal request? Open an issue at github.com/curarda/harmonic-analysis/issues.
