HARMONIC ANALYSIS

What’s stored

Plain-language summary of what this tool keeps and why. Last updated on deployment.

What happens in your browser

The two MIDI files you drop are read and analysed entirely on your own device. The files themselves are not uploaded anywhere.

What is saved

When the “keep an anonymous copy” box is ticked (it is on by default), a compact record of that analysis is saved to help improve the tool. It contains:

  • the detected key, mode, chord progression, cadences and local-key regions;
  • melody-vs-chord statistics and the melodic profile;
  • the note data (pitch, start, length) of the melody and chord lines;
  • the file names you gave the MIDIs, and a timestamp.

No account is created and no personal details are requested. No name, email, IP address or location is stored with the record.

How to not be included

Untick the “keep an anonymous copy” box before pressing Analyze. The analysis still runs and is shown to you exactly the same — nothing is saved.

A note on file names

If you would rather not share what you called a file, rename it to something neutral before dropping it in, or untick the box above.

Questions or a removal request? Email crystallizeus@gmail.com and your record will be deleted.
